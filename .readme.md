## Latest Session Highlight (2026-11-08)
Fixed the **Pre-Market Activation (09:10 AM) -> 09:15 AM Stale Option Price Execution Issue** across both **Paper** and **Live** trade modes:
1. **Fyers Option Quote Validation (`FyersDataPollingService` & `MarketDataCache`)**:
   - Tracks pre-market (`< 09:15 AM IST`) option quote snapshots (`lp`, `volume`, `open_price`) and Fyers' `tt` (last trade timestamp).
   - Rejects any option price after `09:15:00 AM` that still reflects yesterday's closing price (`TradedToday == false`) or was fetched before `09:15:00 AM IST`.
   - Automatically flushes pre-market option quotes and off-market candles at `09:15:00 AM IST` and polls every `1s` during `09:14:00 – 09:15:00 AM` for an instant transition at market open.
2. **Opening Stabilization & Strategy Candle Guards (`StrategyEngineService` & `TradingStrategies`)**:
   - Added a `09:15:00 – 09:16:00 AM IST` stabilization window and `StrategyFilterHelper.HasValidTodaySessionCandles` so strategies never fire at `09:15:00 AM` on yesterday's closing candles.
   - Removed the `150m` fallback price on order entries — if a verified live today's option LTP is unavailable, the entry is cleanly skipped and the strategy state lock is reset.
3. **Live Mode Broker-First Execution & Fill Price Sync (`OrderManagementService` & `OrdersController`)**:
   - In `Live` mode, Fyers orders (`PlaceOrderAsync`) are executed and verified *before* recording local position changes, and `BuyAvg` / `TargetPrice` / `StopLossPrice` are synced with Fyers' actual `buyAvg` from `GetPositionsAsync()`.
